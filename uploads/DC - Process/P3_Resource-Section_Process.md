# Your Resource Section — Process Script
**Pillar:** Process · Block 3 of 11
**Audience:** Account executives and agency staff — internal
**Format:** Motion graphics over real screen capture · AI voice read (two voices) · animated text · green callout boxes
**Runtime:** 1706 counted spoken words ≈ **11:22** at 150 wpm
**Spine — the one sentence somebody must repeat cold:** *If you know where you are in the sale, you know which folder you are in.*

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES — READ BEFORE RECORDING

**Two voices.** **V1 — Guide** carries the teaching. **V2 — Rep** asks what the viewer is thinking. Distinctly different ElevenLabs voice.

**Written for an AI read.** Short sentences, no semicolons, no parentheticals.

**The humor is recognition, not jokes.** Nothing depends on a pause or a dry delivery.

**Visuals: this module lives or dies on screen capture.** Every folder named should be visible on screen as it is spoken, with a **green box** on it. The viewer should be able to follow along in a second window. **No illustrations, no icon cards, no mascots.**

**Deliberate craft note.** This script does **not** name every folder. Naming twenty folders in a row produces a list nobody retains. It teaches the three-step structure, then highlights the handful of things reps most often do not know exist. The complete folder inventory is in the Trainer Notes as a reference sheet instead.

## SEGMENT 1 — WHERE THIS FITS

**ON-SCREEN:** Real screen capture of the resource section home page. Then the three step folders animate with a **green box** landing on each in turn.

**V2 — Rep:** *"Where do I find the… thing. The form. For the audit."*

**V1 — Guide:** Everybody has that sentence at some point. And the good news is that after this, you will not.

**V1 — Guide:** Your **resource section** is where everything lives. Every form, every tool, every presentation, every report. It runs from prospecting a business you have never spoken to, all the way through to reporting on a live campaign.

**V1 — Guide:** And here is the thing that makes it easy, which almost nobody notices on their first visit. **It is not organized alphabetically. It is organized by the sale.**

**V1 — Guide:** The folders at the top are labelled step one, step two, step three. Those are not filing categories. They are the three stages of every deal you will ever do.

**V1 — Guide:** So the line to remember is this. **If you know where you are in the sale, you know which folder you are in.** That is the whole navigation system.

**V1 — Guide:** One other thing about the layout. Almost everything on this page is built for **selling.** The business-side tools — brand assets, revenue modelling, forecasting — sit behind a separate owner login at the bottom. So if something feels like it should be here and is not, that is usually where it went.

## SEGMENT 2 — WHY THIS STEP EXISTS

**ON-SCREEN:** A real branded one-sheet, a real presentation deck, a real calculator — each appearing with a **green box**. Text builds: *already made · already branded · already yours.*

**V1 — Guide:** So why does this get its own block, instead of just sending you a link?

**V1 — Guide:** Two reasons. The first one will save you time. The second one will save you from building something you already own.

**V1 — Guide:** Because here is what happens when somebody has not been shown around. They make their own one-sheet. They build their own presentation. They put together a spreadsheet to work out what a campaign delivers. All of it careful, all of it well-intentioned, and all of it already sitting in a folder, finished, with their own group's brand on it.

**V2 — Rep:** *"So most of what I would need is already in there."*

**V1 — Guide:** Almost all of it. Presentations, one-sheets, calculators, graphics, logos. Branded to your group, ready to use, nothing to build.

**V1 — Guide:** And the second reason. When you know what is in there, you stop guessing in front of clients. There is a tool for the audit, a form for the needs analysis, a package for the bundle, and a report for the month. You are not improvising. You are following a process that already works.

## SEGMENT 3 — THE WALKTHROUGH

**ON-SCREEN:** Real screen capture throughout, following the path being described, **green box** on each element as it is named.

**V1 — Guide:** Let us walk it. Logging in, the three steps, and then the four sections underneath.

### Getting in

**ON-SCREEN:** Green box on the *Resources* link, then the login fields.

**V1 — Guide:** From your group's website, click **Resources**. Then enter your group name and your password.

**V1 — Guide:** Two things about that password. It is **case sensitive**, so type it carefully. And it **changes every ninety days**, which catches people out. Your manager or owner has the current one, so if it stops working, that is who to ask. Nothing is broken.

### Step one — before you pitch

**ON-SCREEN:** Green box on the step one folder, then on the CNA folder and the Sales Process folder.

**V1 — Guide:** Step one is short. Two items, and that is on purpose, because step one is just getting ready.

**V1 — Guide:** **Client needs analysis forms.** That is the blank form. The conversation you have with a business to understand what they actually need. It has its own block later, so we will not go deep now.

**V1 — Guide:** And a **sales process** folder, which explains something important. There are **two ways to sell.**

**V1 — Guide:** **Path one.** You do the needs analysis, you know what they need, and you pitch it directly. No recommendation required.

**V1 — Guide:** **Path two.** You do the needs analysis and send it to our strategy team, who build a tailored recommendation for you. That is the request for recommendation, and it has its own block too.

**V1 — Guide:** Both are legitimate. Which one you use depends on the deal, and that folder walks through how to decide.

### Step two — building the pitch

**ON-SCREEN:** Green box moving across the step two folders, pausing on the Digital Audit Tool, Client Forms, and Sales Packages.

**V1 — Guide:** Step two is where almost everything lives. Seven items, and this is where you will spend most of your time. Let me walk the ones you will use first.

**V1 — Guide:** Two research tools sit at the top. The **digital audit**, which analyzes a business's whole online presence. And **MRR prospect research**, which grades a business as a prospect before you have even spoken to them. Both get their own blocks, because both are genuinely powerful and both take a minute to learn.

**V1 — Guide:** Next, and this one confuses people, so listen. **Client needs analysis, completed, and request for recommendation.**

**V2 — Rep:** *"Hang on. Was the needs analysis not in step one?"*

**V1 — Guide:** Good catch, and yes. The **blank form** is in step one, because that is where you pick it up. The **completed form** goes back in through step two, along with your request for a recommendation. Same document, two different moments.

**V1 — Guide:** Then **client forms**, which is where your presentation material lives. One-sheets for every single tactic, plus a customizable presentation deck. All of it already branded to your group. You are not making slides.

**V1 — Guide:** **IO and intake forms** are also here for now, and we will come back to that in a moment.

**V1 — Guide:** Then **packages**, which is the one people miss. **Pre-built bundles of tactics** that work well together. So instead of assembling a blended recommendation from a blank page, you start from something that already makes sense and adjust it.

**V2 — Rep:** *"So I do not have to figure out which products go together?"*

**V1 — Guide:** That work is done. Start from a package and tailor it.

**V1 — Guide:** And **onsite lead generation**, which is about turning your own website into something that produces leads. Worth twenty minutes when you have them.

### Step three — closing it

**ON-SCREEN:** Green box on the step three folder, then a cut to the online insertion order form at its own link.

**V1 — Guide:** Step three has exactly one thing in it. **The insertion order.** That is where sold business gets submitted so fulfillment can start.

**V1 — Guide:** And one honest note, because you will notice it. The insertion order has moved to **its own online form at its own link**, and we are mid-migration. So for the moment you will see it in step three and you will also see IO and intake forms back in step two.

**V2 — Rep:** *"So which one do I use?"*

**V1 — Guide:** Step three, and bookmark the form itself separately. It is not the same address as this site. It gets its own block, so you will know exactly how it works.

### Scroll down — the featured content

**ON-SCREEN:** Green box on the Digital Blueprint player, then on More Episodes. Then on the featured training block and Download PDF.

**V1 — Guide:** Keep scrolling and two things are sitting front and centre, because they change often.

**V1 — Guide:** **The Digital Blueprint** is our podcast, and the newest episode is always right there. It is not just product information — it is how to actually present each thing to a client. If you have a pitch on Thursday, there is a decent chance there is an episode about exactly that. And **More Episodes** opens the whole library.

**V1 — Guide:** Below that is **featured training**, with the current video and usually a PDF you can download and keep. That is where new products and platform changes get explained first. If something has changed, that is where you will find out about it.

### The bottom sections

**ON-SCREEN:** Green box moving across the six bottom sections in turn.

**V1 — Guide:** Then six smaller sections along the bottom. Quick tour.

**V1 — Guide:** **Training** holds the onboarding and refresher schedule with the Zoom links, the video training archive, and the details for this year's Digital Summit.

**V1 — Guide:** **Reports** is where your client reports live, organized by market and then by client.

**V1 — Guide:** **Trello** is a direct link to your board. That is where all communication with our team happens, and it is the next block.

**V1 — Guide:** **The cost-per-click and cost-per-thousand calculator**, which works out what a campaign actually delivers. Quietly one of the most useful things on the page, and almost nobody finds it on their own.

**V1 — Guide:** **Case studies**, which are real examples of campaigns and what they produced. Take one into a meeting. A story about a business like theirs does more than any statistic.

**V1 — Guide:** And **owners and operators**, which is a separate password-protected login. That is where the business-side tools live — **the brand assets for your digital agency**, including logo files, social graphics, and display ads. **The hosting and website revenue calculator.** A **sales forecasting tool.** And the **employee update form**, for adding and removing staff email addresses.

**V2 — Rep:** *"So that is not for me?"*

**V1 — Guide:** Probably not day to day. But know it is there, because if you need a logo file or you are curious what the forecasting looks like, your owner or manager can get it for you in about a minute. You are not blocked. You just go through them.

## SEGMENT 5 — WHERE THIS GOES WRONG

**ON-SCREEN:** Each mistake appears as text beside the real screen element it relates to.

**V1 — Guide:** Five things, and none of them are dramatic.

**V1 — Guide:** **One. Assuming the password is broken.** It changes every ninety days. Ask your manager before you ask anybody else.

**V1 — Guide:** **Two. Building something that already exists.** Before you make a one-sheet, a deck, or a spreadsheet, look. It is genuinely faster to check than to build.

**V1 — Guide:** **Three. Using an old download.** If you saved a presentation to your desktop eight months ago, it is eight months old. Materials get updated. Pull a fresh copy each time.

**V1 — Guide:** **Four. Never going back.** The Digital Blueprint updates weekly and the training schedule changes. People visit once during onboarding and never return, then wonder why they missed something.

**V1 — Guide:** **Five. Never scrolling past the three steps.** The calculator, the case studies, the podcast, the training — all of it is below the fold, and plenty of people never go looking.

**V1 — Guide:** And the handoff is simple. Everything you just saw feeds into one place. When you have a question, when you submit an order, when our team needs something from you — that all happens on your Trello board. Which is exactly where we are going next.
