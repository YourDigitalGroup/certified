# GBP Module — Voice-over Scripts

Generate one audio file per script (ElevenLabs or Artlist), export as MP3,
and save into `uploads/vo/` with these exact filenames. The portal picks
them up automatically and only falls back to the browser voice if a file
is missing.

## gbp-1.mp3 — What it is
Online Visibility, module five: Google Business Profile. A Google Business Profile highlights your business in local search results, the knowledge panel, and Google Maps — reaching customers actively searching for your products or services. An optimized profile ensures accurate information and maximizes visibility on the world's largest search engine. People can easily interact with the listing to call, get directions, visit the website, or ask questions — driving more engagement and conversions.

## gbp-2.mp3 — Why clients buy it
Why clients buy it. They get found by customers actively searching for their business. They build trust and credibility with verified information and customer reviews. They boost local SEO by signaling relevance to Google's algorithm. And they gain insight into how customers find and interact with the listing.

## gbp-3.mp3 — Anatomy of a listing
The anatomy of a listing. Know these four zones cold. Essential info: accurate details like phone number and hours. Photos and logos: the business's personality. Maps: being found by customers trying to find the location. And reviews: replying builds trust with new and returning customers.

## gbp-4.mp3 — The package
The package: Business Pro. A three hundred dollar one-time setup fee, with no recurring charge. It covers initial profile setup, optimization for search, accurate business information, enhanced features like services, products, and photos, Google verification assistance, and control over the profile.

CLIENT (male voice): "How is this different from SEO?"
NARRATOR: SEO earns rankings over time. GBP is the local layer — Maps, the knowledge panel, reviews. They compound; they don't compete.
